# 💿 Webz

### The internet, the way you remember it.

**Webz** is a throwback to the early days of the internet — a place where anyone can make a website, publish it, and explore websites made by other people.

No fancy web-development degree required. No need to know everything about HTML. Just open Webz, start creating, and see where it takes you.

---

## 🖥️ Welcome to Webz

Imagine turning on an old computer after school.

The desktop loads.

You open the browser.

Instead of Google, social media, or a giant modern website, you find a little world made by **other people just like you.**

Someone made a page about their favorite game.

Someone made a weird website about cats.

Someone built a personal homepage.

Someone made an entire site just because they thought it would be funny.

That's Webz.

**A little internet where people make the internet.**

---

## 🌐 Make Your Own Website

Webz lets you create your own websites and give them a name.

You could make:

* 🎮 A gaming website
* 🎨 An art gallery
* 💻 An HTML experiment
* 📖 A personal homepage
* 🎵 A music-themed site
* 😂 A completely ridiculous website
* 🌎 A site about something you're interested in
* ❓ Literally whatever you can think of

Your website doesn't have to be serious.

It doesn't have to be perfect.

It just has to be **yours.**
